LEHD EMPLOYMENT DATA (LODES & QWI)
====================================
Place the LEHD workplace and employment files for this case in this folder:

1. *_lodes_*.csv
   - Annual workplace employment statistics by industry, age, and earnings tiers.
   - Contains columns:
     [year, jobs_total, age_29_or_younger, age_30_to_54, age_55_or_older,
      earnings_1250_or_less_per_month, earnings_1251_to_3333_per_month,
      earnings_over_3333_per_month, ...]

2. *_qwi_*.csv (or *_qwi_*.xlsx)
   - Quarterly Workforce Indicators (QWI) dataset.
   - Contains quarterly payroll, employment, hires, and separations by year-quarter.
